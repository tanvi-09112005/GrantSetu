CENTER INFORMATION AND HISTORICAL BENEFICIARIES COUNT
Center: Vile Parle Municipal Secondary School, Vile Parle Centre, Mumbai
Background:
Each One Teach One (EOTO) started supporting Vile Parle Municipal Secondary School in 1990 with the aim of providing quality education to underprivileged students. Over the past 34 years, EOTO has implemented various programs to enhance academic performance, digital literacy, and foundational skills.

Program 1: Remedial Classes (Since 1990)
In 1990, remedial classes were introduced for 8th, 9th, and 10th-grade students facing academic difficulties. Over the years, the remedial program has benefited approximately 2,100 students, helping them build a stronger academic foundation and achieve higher secondary passing results.

Program 2: School Support Program (2001 – 2019)
EOTO introduced the School Support Program to provide additional academic assistance in Math, Science, EVS, and Marathi. This program ran for 18 years, benefiting 2,250 students and significantly improving their academic outcomes.

Program 3: Computer Teaching Program (Since 2015)
Recognizing the growing importance of digital literacy, EOTO launched a computer teaching program in 2015. So far, 3,500 students have learned basic computer operations and digital tools, equipping them with essential technological skills.

Program 4: FLN (Foundational Literacy & Numeracy) Program (Since 2022)
The FLN program was introduced in 2022 to address learning gaps caused by the COVID-19 pandemic. Many students struggled with reading, writing, and mathematics. This program has benefited 450 students, strengthening their foundational literacy and numeracy skills.

Program 5: Holistic Development Programs
In addition to academic support, EOTO has implemented various initiatives to promote overall student development:
- Personality Development Sessions: 5 sessions per year enhancing self-awareness and opinion expression.
- Exposure Visits: Bus, food, and venue-supported experiential learning visits beyond textbooks.
- Sports Olympiad & Annual Sports Day: Athletics, teamwork, confidence, and participation.
- Annual Functions & Cultural Events: Dance teacher charges, script xerox, costumes, and snacks.
- Rapport Building Activities: ₹20 per student per month for 48 students over 9 months.
- Skill-based Vocational Training: Creating opportunities for financial independence.
- Psychometric Assessment & Career Guidance: Structured psychometric tests for 10th-grade students (₹1,000 per student for 20 students) to guide career choices and reduce secondary dropout rates.

   Impact: Enhanced digital skills, basic typing, software familiarity, and internet safety.
4. Basic Learning Support:
   Impact: Distributed notebooks (one dozen per child for Remedial; half dozen per child for FLN) ensuring uninterrupted studies.
5. Extracurricular & Rapport Building:
   Impact: 9 annual activities (Yoga Day, essay writing, handwriting, arts & crafts, elocution, reading, greeting cards, drawing, talent hunt, and group projects) boosting confidence and student retention.
6. Psychometric Assessment & Career Guidance:
   Impact: Administered specialized career tests and guidance counseling for 20 secondary students, significantly lowering high-school dropout rates.
